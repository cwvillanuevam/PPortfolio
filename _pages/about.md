## Professional profile

I am a Mechanical Electrical Engineer with a Master of Science and a Doctor of Science, both in Energy. My background combines power-system engineering, energy planning, quantitative modelling, economic evaluation and data analytics.

My professional experience includes electricity-system studies, operational and contingency analysis, regulatory assessment, technical and economic evaluation of energy projects, and the development of analytical tools and reporting workflows. I have worked with engineering consultancies and in the electricity distribution sector, contributing to studies that require the integration of technical, regulatory and economic perspectives.

My academic work has focused on optimisation and simulation of power systems, renewable-energy integration, transmission expansion planning, and energy management for electric-vehicle charging.

## Education

- **Doctor of Science in Energy** — National University of Engineering (UNI), approved with excellence.
- **Master of Science in Energy** — National University of Engineering (UNI), thesis approved with distinction.
- **B.Sc. in Mechanical Electrical Engineering** — National University of Engineering (UNI).

## Professional experience

**Power-system consulting and engineering studies**
- Power-system database analysis and preparation of inputs for medium- and long-term studies.
- Operational, power-flow and contingency studies, including pre-operational assessments.
- Technical and regulatory analysis of generation, ancillary services and pumped-storage hydropower alternatives.
- Technical-economic assessment of energy and infrastructure projects.

**Electricity distribution and service quality**
- Analysis of metering information, technical losses and electricity supply quality.
- Development of operational indicators, management reports and monitoring tools.
- Metering-system configuration, monitoring and support for maintenance and contingency analysis.

## Working approach

I combine engineering judgement with quantitative methods, structured data analysis and reproducible modelling. I am particularly interested in roles that connect energy-sector strategy, power-system analysis, investment assessment and data-driven decision-making.

This portfolio distinguishes professional experience from academic research and presents project descriptions at a level that respects confidentiality and ownership obligations.
