My academic research has focused on power-system optimisation, renewable-energy integration and energy management for electric-vehicle charging. The work combines mathematical modelling, simulation, statistical analysis and technical-economic assessment.

## Doctoral research

### Energy Management Systems for Electric Vehicle Charging in Electric Distribution Networks: Operational Evaluation
**Doctor of Science in Energy | National University of Engineering | 2018–2023**

Research on the design and operational evaluation of energy-management strategies for electric-vehicle charging in distribution networks. The work included statistical analysis, Monte Carlo simulation, data synthesis and power-flow studies to assess charging impacts and distributed-generation alternatives.

## Master's research

### Transmission Expansion Planning under Conditions of Maximum Electricity Generation with Renewable Energy Resources
**Master of Science in Energy | National University of Engineering | 2016–2019**

Research on transmission expansion planning under high renewable-generation penetration. The work used optimisation and power-system analysis, including AC optimal power flow and N-1 contingency assessment, to examine network reinforcement alternatives.

## Selected scientific articles

### Analysis and Synthesis of Data from Electric Vehicle Centralized Charging Management Strategies: Impact on Distribution Systems
**2023**

Research on the generation, analysis and synthesis of distribution-system datasets for evaluating centralised electric-vehicle charging management strategies, including power-flow simulations on a test distribution network.

### Techno-Operational Analysis of Controlled Charging of Electric Vehicles — Interaction between Centralized and Decentralized Charge Management
**2023**

Analysis of interactions between centralised and decentralised charging strategies, using driving-pattern data, energy-management scenarios and power-flow simulation to evaluate network operating conditions.

### Impact of Monte Carlo Simulation of Uncontrolled Charging of Electric Vehicles on Distributed Generation
**2023**

Assessment of uncontrolled electric-vehicle charging using statistical characterisation and Monte Carlo simulation, including evaluation of distributed photovoltaic generation and battery storage as alternatives to address additional demand.

## Research methods and tools

- Mathematical optimisation and power-system modelling.
- Power-flow and contingency analysis.
- Statistical analysis, Monte Carlo simulation and data synthesis.
- Renewable-generation integration and transmission planning.
- Electric-vehicle charging management and distributed energy resources.
