The following examples summarise selected professional engineering and consulting work. They focus on the type of analysis and technical contribution, without disclosing confidential client information or presenting institutional deliverables as personal intellectual property.

## Power-system studies and consulting

### Pre-operational power-system evaluation models
**KIEV Associates | 2022**

- Prepared and managed power-system databases for long-term electrical studies.
- Conducted power-flow analysis to support the validation of pre-operational studies.
- Worked with open-access modelling approaches and structured technical inputs for system evaluation.

### Generation project assessment — Honduras
**KIEV Associates | 2022**

- Supported the technical assessment of a generation project through analysis of the Honduran power-system database.
- Reviewed generation and ancillary-services considerations and relevant regional regulatory alternatives.

### Risk assessment for generation-unit outages
**KIEV Associates | 2022–2023**

- Analysed publicly available electricity-sector data and system-planning information.
- Developed alternative generation scenarios to assess the potential effects of generation-unit outages.
- Used generation modelling to support risk assessment and decision-making.

### Regulatory assessment of pumped-storage hydropower
**KIEV Associates | 2023**

- Compared regulatory and legislative frameworks from Honduras, Chile and Peru.
- Contributed to a proposal for adapting regulatory provisions for pumped-storage hydropower to the local context.

### Electricity project feasibility assessment
**Independent consulting | 2022**

- Managed and analysed electricity-sector databases for a concession-area assessment.
- Supported medium- and long-term investment perspectives and supplier evaluation.
- Contributed to the technical feasibility assessment of the project.
