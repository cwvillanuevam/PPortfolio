## Engineering, modelling and evidence-based analysis

I am a Mechanical Electrical Engineer with a Master of Science and a Doctor of Science, both in Energy. My professional and research experience brings together power-system analysis, energy planning, quantitative modelling, economic evaluation and data analytics.

My work has involved technical and economic assessments of electricity systems, operational and contingency studies, regulatory analysis, energy-management strategies and the development of analytical tools. My academic research has focused on power-system optimisation, renewable-energy integration, transmission planning and electric-vehicle charging management.

## Areas of expertise

- **Energy planning and markets:** scenario analysis, generation and transmission planning, electricity-market analysis and regulatory assessment.
- **Power systems:** power-flow studies, operational analysis, contingencies, distributed generation and renewable-energy integration.
- **Quantitative modelling:** optimisation, simulation, statistical analysis and Monte Carlo methods.
- **Economic evaluation:** project feasibility and the assessment of investment and operating alternatives.
- **Data analytics and automation:** data preparation, analysis, reporting, process automation and business intelligence.
